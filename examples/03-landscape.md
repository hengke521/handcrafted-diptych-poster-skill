# Landscape: coastal cliff and path

## Hypothetical reference

A chalk-colored headland with a curved footpath, deep blue sea, and a pale horizon. The land projects diagonally from the lower left toward the center.

## Skill invocation

```text
Use $handcrafted-diptych-poster-skill with the attached coastal image.
Create a 2:3 vertical diptych, original above and a tiny paper landscape below.
Keep the diagonal headland and curved path. Use cardboard, hand-cut paper,
and one piece of string. Miniature area 24%; high negative space; no text.
```

## Lower-panel generation prompt

```text
Create only a horizontal 4:3 lower panel, conditioned on the attached coastal
reference. Build a shallow handcrafted stage from a chalk-colored torn-edge
cardboard headland, one deep-blue hand-cut paper sea plane, and a short thin
cream string following the curved footpath. Preserve the reference's diagonal
land projection, path curve, and land-to-sea color contrast. Compress the distant
horizon into the edge of the blue plane; do not paint a full scenic background.

The whole stage fits a bounding rectangle about 60% of the panel width and
40% of its height, approximately 24% area. Center at 50% across, 62% down.
Use uninterrupted pale cool-gray space outside the stage. Show visible cardboard
layers, paper thickness, fine string fibers, and slight hand-cut unevenness.
Use soft daylight from the same side as the reference, gentle contact shadows,
and restrained blue/chalk/cool-gray colors. Photograph of an editorial maquette.
No text, borders, or decorative clouds.

Avoid a full-size scenic diorama, waves covering the panel, cartoon ocean,
glitter, glossy resin water, plastic cliffs, smooth CGI surfaces, excessive
colors, oversized subject, extra panels, pseudo-text, watermarks, template graphics.
```

## Assembly

Use the original in the upper half, fitted without losing the tip of the headland. Keep the lower ground mostly empty: the sea is a small stage component, not a full-panel background.

## Integrated-route alternative

Use only when an editorial re-render of the upper image is acceptable:

```text
Create a vertical 2:3 art poster with two equal-height stacked panels.
Above: a faithful editorial reinterpretation of the attached coastal photograph,
retaining its diagonal chalk headland, curved path, deep-blue sea, pale horizon,
and lighting. Below: a tiny physical maquette of that headland using layered
chalk cardboard, one hand-cut blue paper sea plane, and a cream string path.
Its bounding box occupies about 24% of the lower panel, centered horizontally
and slightly below center on a pale cool-gray ground. Keep abundant empty space.
Use visible paper edges, cardboard layers, fine fibers, matte surfaces, and
soft contact shadows. Independent art-publication restraint. No text.
Avoid extra panels, toy styling, glossy plastic, polished CGI, large scenery,
clutter, pseudo-text, watermarks, and template graphics.
```

Label the result as a re-render; do not describe the upper panel as the untouched original.
