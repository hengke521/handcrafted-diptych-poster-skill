A pale stone courtyard with one tall arch, a terracotta pot to its right, a dark recess behind it, and strong afternoon light from the left.

## Assembly

Place the original above the generated panel, each occupying half the height. Contain-fit the original on a stone-compatible matte. Retain the arch's full height. If the miniature becomes a generic façade, restore the arch's original proportions and the pot's offset before adding texture.
