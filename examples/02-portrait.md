```text

Avoid chibi proportions, doll advertising, button eyes, caricature, glossy skin,
```

## Assembly

Contain-fit the original portrait into the upper panel. Keep all visible pose-defining limbs in frame. The lower abstraction preserves gesture and clothing cues; it does not promise biometric likeness. If it reads as a toy, correct head proportions and facial styling before changing materials.
